---
title: Delete field
openapi: "v2 delete /datasets/{dataset_id}/fields/{field_id}"
---

<Note>
When the endpoint returns `202`, the field deletion job is queued and runs asynchronously. The deletion itself can take several hours to complete. For more information, see [Delete fields](/reference/datasets#delete-fields).

If another job blocks the request, Axiom returns `409 Conflict`. When available, `blockingJobs` lists the active job IDs and their types. Wait for those jobs to finish before retrying. An organization under legal hold also receives `409 Conflict`.
</Note>
