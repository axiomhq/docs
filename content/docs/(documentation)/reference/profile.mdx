## Change name

1. Click <Icon icon="gear" iconType="solid"/> **Settings > Profile**.
1. Enter your name in the **Name** section.

## View contact details and base role

1. Click <Icon icon="gear" iconType="solid"/> **Settings > Profile**.
1. Find your your contact details and base role in the **Email** and **Role** sections.

## Change timezone

1. Click <Icon icon="gear" iconType="solid"/> **Settings > Profile**.
1. Select your timezone in the **Timezone** section.

## Change editor mode

The editor mode determines the style of the APL query editor. To change this:

1. Click <Icon icon="gear" iconType="solid"/> **Settings > Profile**.
1. Select your preferred editor in the **Editor mode** section.

## Select default method for null values

When you visualize your data, you can select how Axiom treats missing or undefined values in the chart. For more information, see [Configure dashboard elements](/dashboard-elements/configure#values). When you select a default method to deal with null values, Axiom uses this method in every new chart you create.

To select the default method for null values:

1. Click <Icon icon="gear" iconType="solid"/> **Settings > Profile**.
1. Select your preferred editor in the **default method for null values** section.

## View query usage

The **Query usage** section shows your remaining hourly and daily query cost limits inherited from your roles.

1. Click <Icon icon="gear" iconType="solid"/> **Settings > Profile**.
1. View your remaining hourly and daily query cost limits in the **Query usage** section.

For more information, see [Set query cost limits for AI agents](/console/intelligence/query-cost-limits).

## Manage personal access tokens

Create and delete personal access tokens (PATs). For more information, see [Personal access tokens](/reference/tokens#personal-access-tokens-pat).

## View and manage active sessions

1. Click <Icon icon="gear" iconType="solid"/> **Settings > Profile**.
1. View active browser sessions in the **Sessions** section.
1. Optional: To log out of another session, find it in the list, and then click <Icon icon="trash" iconType="light"/> on the right.

## Manage connected apps

The **Connected apps** section lists OAuth apps you authorized to access Axiom as you, such as [MCP clients](/console/intelligence/mcp-server).

To revoke an app’s access:

1. Click <Icon icon="gear" iconType="solid"/> **Settings > Profile**.
1. In **Connected apps**, find the app, click <Icon icon="trash" iconType="light"/> on the right, and then click **Revoke access**.

Revoking an app ends its access across all devices within 5 minutes.

## Delete user account

<Warning>
This is a destructive action. After you delete your user account, you can’t recover it.
</Warning>

To delete your user account:

1. Click <Icon icon="gear" iconType="solid"/> **Settings > Profile**.
1. Click **Delete account**.
