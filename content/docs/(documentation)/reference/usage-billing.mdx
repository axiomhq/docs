## Billing

To view details about your next and last bill, go to <Icon icon="gear" iconType="solid"/> **Settings > Billing**.

The Current breakdown section explains what you paid for in your last bill and what you can expect to pay for in your next bill.

Axiom Cloud includes a $25 per month platform fee plus usage charges. Axiom can also issue mid-cycle invoices when your usage reaches your plan’s billing threshold.

### View billing history

To view details about your billing history:

1. Go to <Icon icon="gear" iconType="solid"/> **Settings > Billing**.
1. In the Current breakdown section, click **Billing history**.

### Compute credits

The Compute credits section is available on the Axiom Cloud plan.

You can save on compute spending by pre-purchasing compute credits. This means that you purchase credits in advance at a discount. The more credits you purchase, the higher the discount rate. This commitment discount applies on top of the automatic volume discounts on usage. Compute credits don’t expire. For more information, see [Pricing](https://axiom.co/pricing).

Your compute credits can cover the following costs:
- Data loading
- Query compute

Your compute credits can’t cover the following costs:
- Your pricing plan
- Add-ons
- Storage

You can’t buy compute credits in the Axiom Console. To pre-purchase compute credits, email [sales@axiom.co](mailto:sales@axiom.co) with the volume you want to purchase. To get help choosing the right volume for your usage, ask in the same email.

To view the discount for each volume of credits:

1. Go to <Icon icon="gear" iconType="solid"/> **Settings > Billing**.
1. In the Compute credits section, click **Manage credits**.

The Pre-purchase discounts table shows the discount for each volume of credits. To email sales, click **Contact sales**.

If your organization has compute credits, the Compute credits section in <Icon icon="gear" iconType="solid"/> **Settings > Billing** also shows your credit balance, your average daily credit usage in the current billing period, and the date of your last purchase.

#### Bill through AWS Marketplace

To pay for compute credits through AWS Marketplace instead of being invoiced directly by Axiom:

1. Ensure you have permissions to modify billing. For more information, see [Role-Based Access Control](/reference/settings).
1. Go to <Icon icon="gear" iconType="solid"/> **Settings > Billing**.
1. In the Compute credits section, click **Manage credits**.
1. In the Bill through AWS Marketplace section, enter your 12-digit AWS account ID, and then click **Save**.
1. When you contact sales, include your AWS account ID. Axiom then sets up a private offer for you in AWS Marketplace.

### Spending limit

Setting a monthly spending limit allows you to control costs.

<Warning>
Axiom continuously tracks your current billing cycle’s projected costs. To determine your accumulated charges, Axiom considers all costs, including the base cost of your plan, usage costs, and the costs of any add-ons you have purchased. When your accumulated charges reach your spending limit, Axiom automatically pauses actions that generate further costs. This can cause interruptions to your service.

For example, if you have used up the query compute allowance included in your pricing plan, you can’t run queries and your monitors can’t query your data. At the same time, if you haven’t yet used up the data loading allowance, you can still ingest data.
</Warning>

Even if you reach your spending limit, you can still initiate purchases manually such as buying add-ons.

The spending limit applies to your whole organization. To cap the query costs of individual API tokens or members instead, for example for AI agents, set [query cost limits](/console/intelligence/query-cost-limits) on API tokens and roles.

To set a monthly spending limit:

1. Ensure you have permissions to modify billing. For more information, see [Role-Based Access Control](/reference/settings).
1. Go to <Icon icon="gear" iconType="solid"/> **Settings > Billing**.
1. In the **Spending limit** section, turn on **Limit monthly spending**.
1. Enter a **Monthly limit**.
1. Click **Save limit**.

<Info>
If your organization reaches the spend limit, Axiom sends an email to the owner of your organization. To avoid further interruptions to your service, increase the spend limit. Your service resumes until usage-related costs reach the amount by which you increased the limit. The spend limit resets in the next billable period as usual.
</Info>

## Pricing plan

### View plan details

To view details about your current plan:

1. Go to <Icon icon="gear" iconType="solid"/> **Settings > Plan**.
1. Click **License**.

This page gives you an overview of the allowances included in your plan and your current monthly usage.

### Upgrade or downgrade plan

To upgrade or downgrade your plan:

1. Ensure you have permissions to modify billing. For more information, see [Role-Based Access Control](/reference/settings).
1. Go to <Icon icon="gear" iconType="solid"/> **Settings > Plan**.
   - To upgrade, click **Upgrade now** under the plan you want and complete checkout.
   - To downgrade an eligible plan, click <Icon icon="ellipsis-vertical" iconType="solid"/> **Plan actions > Downgrade**.

### Manage add-ons

You can manage the following add-ons:

- Additional edge deployments
- [Audit log](/reference/audit-log)
- [Directory Sync](/reference/settings#directory-sync)
- [Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)](/reference/settings)
- [SAML Single Sign-On (SSO)](/reference/settings#single-sign-on-saml-sso)

To manage add-ons:

1. Ensure you have permissions to modify billing. For more information, see [Role-Based Access Control](/reference/settings).
1. Go to <Icon icon="gear" iconType="solid"/> **Settings > Plan**.
1. In the **Add-ons** section, click **Manage add-ons** to open the add-ons sidebar.
1. Select or deselect add-ons.
1. Review the updated cost.
1. Click **Update add-ons...**, and then click **Confirm**.

## Usage

To view details about your organization’s total usage of Axiom:

1. Click <Icon icon="gear" iconType="solid"/> **Settings > Usage**.
1. Optional: Filter by edge deployment if your organization uses multiple edge deployments.
1. To select the period for which to display usage, choose a billing period or time range (`30d`, `90d`, `365d`, or a custom range).

The Usage page displays summary totals for **Data loading**, **Query compute**, and **Average storage**, followed by detailed breakdowns:

- **Data loading**: Data loaded into Axiom, grouped by **Dataset**.
- **Rejected data**: Appears when Axiom couldn’t store some incoming events or metrics data points during the selected period. Lists each affected dataset, rejection reasons (such as timestamps outside the allowed range, events that are too large or exceed memory limits, or invalid requests), and rejected volume (bytes for event datasets, data points for metrics datasets). Rejected bytes count toward data loading. Partial-ingest warnings where Axiom stores an event and drops only excess new fields past the [dataset field limit](/reference/limits#restrictions-on-datasets-and-fields) aren’t counted as failed events.
- **Query compute**: Query compute resources used to run queries, grouped by **Dataset**.
- **MCP query compute**: Query compute attributed to [Axiom MCP Server](/console/intelligence/mcp-server) clients, grouped by **User** or **Dataset**. Available when viewing all edge deployments.
- **Storage**: Data stored in your datasets, grouped by **Dataset** or **Access tier** (see [Adaptive tiering](/reference/optimize-usage#adaptive-tiering)).

### Monitor usage in detail

For more granular usage monitoring, query the [audit log](/reference/audit-log). The audit log captures hourly usage data and allows you to:

- Track ingestion by dataset over time.
- Identify high-cost queries.
- Monitor which monitors consume the most query resources.
- Detect ingestion spikes.
