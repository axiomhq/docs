- **“Products and Services”** means the products and services listed in the Program Requirements.
- **“Program Requirements”** means the Axiom Partner Program Guide and related terms and conditions available at the following URL: https://axiom.co/docs/legal/partner-program-guide.

- **“Qualified Lead”** means a Prospect that meets the Program Requirements, as confirmed by Axiom in writing.
- **“Referral Fee”** means the fees payable to Partner pursuant to Sections 3 and 4.
- **“Referral Period”** means the applicable period of time specified in the Program Requirements.

## 3. Referral Qualification Process

**3.1	Prospects.** Partner may notify Axiom with respect to a  Prospect according to the process set forth in the Program Requirements.  Axiom will promptly review such request and respond regarding the same either confirming the Prospect has met the Program Requirements or has not.  
**3.2	Qualified Leads and Referral Activities.** If a Prospect becomes a Qualified Lead, Partner agrees to assist such Qualified Lead in the pre-sales process. Partner will, if requested by Axiom, perform any of the following  additional activities with respect to such Qualified Lead: provide Axiom with the Qualified Lead’s business background; organize and participate in meetings between Axiom and the Qualified Lead; and assist Axiom’s sales efforts to the Qualified Lead.   
**3.3	Customers.** If a Qualified Lead becomes an Axiom subscriber of the corresponding Product or Service within the applicable Referral Period, then that Qualified Lead shall become a Customer. If the Qualified Lead does not subscribe to the corresponding Product or Service before the Referral Period expires, and if the parties do not mutually agree in writing to extend the Referral Period, that Qualified Lead will revert into a Prospect. Partner will not be entitled to any fee with respect to a Prospect, whether or not Axiom subsequently enters into an agreement with such Prospect or such Prospect subscribes to any Product or Service.

## 4. Referral Fees

If Axiom receives and books Fees from a Customer within the applicable Referral Period, then, subject to all other provisions of this Agreement, Axiom shall pay to Partner a Referral Fee in accordance with the Program Requirements. The Referral Fee shall be due and payable in accordance with the payout terms set out in the Program Requirements, including any holding period, payout cycle, and minimum payout threshold specified therein. If Partner receives a Referral Fee from Axiom under this Agreement, Partner may not receive a referral, finders or similar fee from Axiom with respect to the same transaction under any other agreement or arrangement. Partner agrees to reimburse Axiom for any overpaid amounts.
