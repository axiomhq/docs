## Disable notifiers

Disable a notifier to stop notifications for a specific amount of time.

To disable a notifier:

1. In Axiom, click **Notifiers** in the sidebar.
1. Click the notifier in the list that you want to disable.
1. Click **Disable notifier**.
1. Select the time period for which you want to disable the notifier.
1. Click **Disable**.

Axiom automatically enables the notifier after the time period you specified.

## Enable notifiers

To enable a notifier:

1. In Axiom, click **Notifiers** in the sidebar.
1. Click the notifier in the list that you want to enable.
1. If you edited the notifier, save or discard your changes.
1. In **Status**, click **Enable**.

## Test notifiers

In the notifier settings, click **Send test** to send a notification using the values in the form, including unsaved changes. The test leaves the notifier’s settings and status unchanged. For a saved notifier, the triggered and resolved test notifications appear in **Deliveries** as **Test**.

## Troubleshoot notifier deliveries

Open **Notifiers** in the sidebar and select a saved notifier. **Deliveries** shows attempts from the last 7 days, newest first.

If the notifier is **Failing**, check how many deliveries failed in a row and when one last succeeded. Find the failed delivery by monitor name and time. Test notifications appear as **Test**. Check the HTTP status code when available and expand **Error** for details.

To inspect notification errors for a specific monitor run, see [View monitor status](/monitor-data/view-monitor-status).

## Delete notifiers

To delete a notifier:

1. In Axiom, click **Notifiers** in the sidebar.
1. Click the notifier in the list that you want to delete.
1. In the top right, click <Icon icon="trash" iconType="light"/>.
1. Click **Delete**.
