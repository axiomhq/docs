## Edit monitors

To edit an existing monitor:

1. Click the Monitors tab.
1. Click the monitor in the list that you want to edit.
1. In the top right, click **Edit monitor**.
1. Make changes to the monitor.
1. Click **Save**.

## Show annotations in monitor charts

Threshold and anomaly monitor charts display annotations for the monitor’s dataset and the chart’s time range.

On a monitor detail page, open **Annotations** beside the time range in **Recent activity**. Select individual types or use **All annotations**. On phones, open **Recent activity options** (`⋯`), then select **Time range** or **Annotations**.

While editing a monitor, run the preview query. Expand **Chart** in the settings panel. In **Annotations**, click **Manage**. If the panel is hidden, click **Settings**. Visibility choices affect the current view and are not saved with the monitor.

For supported charts, type search, and display limits, see [Annotations in monitors](/query-data/annotate-charts#show-and-hide-annotations-in-monitors).

## Disable monitors

Disable a monitor to prevent it from running for a specific amount of time.

To disable a monitor:

1. Click the Monitors tab.
1. Click the monitor in the list that you want to disable.
1. In the top right, click **Disable monitor**.
1. Select the time period for which you want to disable the monitor.
1. Click **Disable monitor**.

Axiom automatically enables the monitor after the time period you specified.

## Enable monitors

To enable a monitor:

1. Click the Monitors tab.
1. Click the monitor in the list that you want to enable.
1. In the top right, click **Enable monitor**.
1. Click **Enable monitor**.

## Clone monitors

To clone a monitor:

1. Click the Monitors tab.
1. Click the monitor in the list that you want to delete.
1. In the top right, click <Icon icon="ellipsis-vertical" iconType="solid"/> **More**.
1. Click **Clone monitor**.

## Delete monitors

To delete a monitor:

1. Click the Monitors tab.
1. Click the monitor in the list that you want to delete.
1. In the top right, click <Icon icon="ellipsis-vertical" iconType="solid"/> **More**.
1. Click **Delete monitor**.
