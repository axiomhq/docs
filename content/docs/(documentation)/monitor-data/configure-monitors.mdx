## Edit monitors

To edit an existing monitor:

1. Click **Monitors** in the sidebar.
1. Click the monitor in the list that you want to edit.
1. In the top right, click **Edit monitor**.
1. Make changes to the monitor.
1. Click **Save**.

## Data that arrives late

If your data arrives late, set **Delay** in the monitor’s **Evaluation** settings. Enter a whole number of seconds from 0 to 86,400 (24 hours). The delay shifts the time range of each run into the past. For example, with a delay of 15 minutes, a run at 10:15 checks data up to 10:00.

If Axiom suggests a delay for a match monitor, click the suggested value to apply it. The suggestion is based on the dataset’s recent ingestion delay.

## Notification settings

For threshold and anomaly monitors, expand **Notification settings** in the editor.

- **Notify by group** tracks alerts separately for each group in the query results.
- **Repeat alerts** sends a notification on every run that ends in alert.
- **Notify on resolve** sends a notification when an alert closes. If you turn it off, alerts in PagerDuty, Opsgenie, Jira Service Management, and incident.io stay open until you close them there.

## Labels

In the monitor editor, expand **Labels** and select the labels to assign. The changes take effect when you save the monitor. For more information, see [Organize resources with labels](/reference/resource-labels).

## Show annotations in monitor charts

Threshold and anomaly monitors support annotations in **Recent activity** and in chart previews.

For more information, see [Annotations](/annotations#show-and-hide-annotations-in-monitors).

## Disable monitors

Disable a monitor to prevent it from running for a specific amount of time or indefinitely.

To disable a monitor:

1. Click **Monitors** in the sidebar.
1. Click the monitor in the list that you want to disable.
1. In the top right, click **Disable monitor**.
1. Select the time period for which you want to disable the monitor.
1. Click **Disable monitor**.

Axiom automatically enables the monitor after the time period you specified. If you select **Indefinite**, the monitor stays disabled until you enable it.

## Enable monitors

To enable a monitor:

1. Click **Monitors** in the sidebar.
1. Click the monitor in the list that you want to enable.
1. In the top right, click **Enable monitor**.
1. Click **Enable monitor**.

## Clone monitors

To clone a monitor:

1. Click **Monitors** in the sidebar.
1. Click the monitor in the list that you want to clone.
1. In the top right, click <Icon icon="ellipsis-vertical" iconType="solid"/> **Monitor actions**.
1. Click **Clone monitor**.

## Delete monitors

To delete a monitor:

1. Click **Monitors** in the sidebar.
1. Click the monitor in the list that you want to delete.
1. In the top right, click <Icon icon="ellipsis-vertical" iconType="solid"/> **Monitor actions**.
1. Click **Delete monitor**.
