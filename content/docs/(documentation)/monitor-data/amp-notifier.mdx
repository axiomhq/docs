Use an Amp notifier to send monitor alerts to an Amp orb.

To create an Amp notifier, follow these steps:

1. Create an Amp webhook that accepts a JSON body with a `text` field. For more information, see the [Amp documentation](https://ampcode.com/docs/orbs/automations#webhooks).
1. In Axiom, click **Notifiers** in the sidebar.
1. Click **New notifier**.
1. Name your notifier.
1. Click **Amp**.
1. In **Webhook URL**, enter the HTTPS URL of the webhook.
1. Click **Create**.
