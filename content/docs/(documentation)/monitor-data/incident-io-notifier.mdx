To create an incident.io notifier, follow these steps:

1. In incident.io, create an HTTP alert source and copy its alert events URL and token. For more information, see the [incident.io documentation](https://docs.incident.io/alerts/custom-http-sources).
1. In Axiom, click **Notifiers** in the sidebar.
1. Click **New notifier**.
1. Name your notifier.
1. Click **incident.io**.
1. In **Alert source ID**, enter the last segment of the alert events URL.
1. In **Token**, enter the token of the HTTP alert source.
1. Click **Create**.
