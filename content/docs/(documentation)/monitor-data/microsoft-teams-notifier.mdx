Use a Microsoft Teams notifier to send a notification to a specific channel in your Microsoft Teams instance.

To create a Microsoft Teams notifier, follow these steps:

1. In Microsoft Teams, create a webhook using the **Send webhook alerts to a channel** template in **Workflows**. For more information, see the [Microsoft documentation](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/workflows/send-messages-in-teams-using-incoming-webhooks).
1. In Axiom, click **Notifiers** in the sidebar.
1. Click **New notifier** on the top right.
1. Name your notifier.
1. Click **Microsoft Teams**.
1. Enter the webhook URL you have previously generated.
1. Click **Create**.
