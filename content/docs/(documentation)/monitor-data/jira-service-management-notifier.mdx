To create a Jira Service Management notifier, follow these steps:

1. In Jira Service Management, create an API integration. For more information, see the [Atlassian documentation](https://support.atlassian.com/jira-service-management-cloud/docs/set-up-an-api-integration/).
1. In Axiom, click **Notifiers** in the sidebar.
1. Click **New notifier**.
1. Name your notifier.
1. Click **Jira Service Management**.
1. In **API key**, enter the API key of the integration.
1. Click **Create**.
