## AI chat

To get an answer about Axiom from the documentation’s built-in AI assistant, press <kbd className="inline-flex h-5 items-center justify-center rounded-sm border border-input bg-secondary px-1.5 align-middle font-mono text-xs leading-none font-medium text-secondary-foreground shadow-none!">Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>&nbsp;<kbd className="inline-flex h-5 items-center justify-center rounded-sm border border-input bg-secondary px-1.5 align-middle font-mono text-xs leading-none font-medium text-secondary-foreground shadow-none!">I</kbd> or click the floating **Ask AI** button in the bottom right, and then type your question. You can also press <kbd className="inline-flex h-5 items-center justify-center rounded-sm border border-input bg-secondary px-1.5 align-middle font-mono text-xs leading-none font-medium text-secondary-foreground shadow-none!">Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>&nbsp;<kbd className="inline-flex h-5 items-center justify-center rounded-sm border border-input bg-secondary px-1.5 align-middle font-mono text-xs leading-none font-medium text-secondary-foreground shadow-none!">K</kbd> to open search and select the **Ask AI** action from the search dialog.

## Interpret individual pages

To interpret individual pages of the documentation with an LLM, click **Copy page** in the top right to copy the page in Markdown format, or open the menu next to it and choose one of the following options:
- **View as Markdown**: Open the raw Markdown for the page.
- **Ask AI about this page**: Open the built-in AI assistant with the current page as context.
- **Open in Claude**: Open the page in Claude.
- **Open in ChatGPT**: Open the page in ChatGPT.
- **Open in Grok**: Open the page in Grok.

## Interpret the full docs

To make it easy for LLMs to interpret the documentation, Axiom offers the following based on the [/llms.txt standard](https://llmstxt.org/):

- [List of docs pages](https://axiom.co/docs/llms.txt)
- [Full documentation](https://axiom.co/docs/llms-full.txt)
- [List of query reference pages](/llms-apl.md)

Access one of the above documents and pass it to your LLM to start interpreting the Axiom documentation.
